Potowhari ⇄ English Voice Interpreter

Tap the mic, speak a phrase, and hear it translated back out loud — in either direction.

Two things to know before you use this:
1. No browser or cloud service ships native speech recognition or text-to-speech for Potowhari (Pahari-Potwari). This app approximates it using the closest available voices (Urdu / Punjabi), which will mispronounce plenty of words — treat it as a rough stand-in, not a native speaker.
2. Translation runs on a small built-in phrasebook (0 entries — mostly hand-transcribed from a scanned Mirpuri/Potwari phrasebook handout, plus a few from web search). Because it came off a photographed page, spelling is approximate, not authoritative. Browse what's available below, or tap any entry to try it instantly.
