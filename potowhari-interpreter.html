Potowhari ⇄ English Voice Interpreter

Speak a sentence, get it translated, hear it read back out loud — in either direction.

Two things to know before you use this:
1. No browser or cloud service ships native speech recognition or text-to-speech for Potowhari (Pahari-Potwari). This app approximates it using the closest available voices (Urdu / Punjabi), which will mispronounce plenty of words — treat it as a rough stand-in, not a native speaker.
2. Translation runs on a small built-in phrasebook (0 entries — greetings and numbers only, gathered from web search and not yet verified against a primary source). Anything outside that list won't be recognized yet. See the PHRASEBOOK array and translateText() in this file's source to extend it or swap in a real translation API.
3. Recording auto-translates and speaks the result out loud as soon as you stop talking — full voice-to-voice, no extra clicks needed.
